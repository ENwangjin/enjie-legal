搜索不到设备怎么办？The device cannot be found

1. 确认电池已上电、BMS 指示灯正常工作；

2. 手机蓝牙已开启，并与设备保持 3 米以内、无金属遮挡；

3. 关闭再重新打开手机蓝牙后重新搜索；

4. 若设备已被其他手机连接，请先在那边断开；

5. Android 11 及以下系统需要允许定位权限才能扫描到蓝牙设备。

1. Make sure the battery is powered on and the BMS indicator is working;

2. Turn on phone Bluetooth and stay within 3 meters without metal obstructions;

3. Toggle Bluetooth off and on, then scan again;

4. If another phone is already connected, disconnect it first;

5. Android 11 and below requires location permission to scan for Bluetooth devices.

为什么需要蓝牙和定位权限？Why Bluetooth and location permissions

蓝牙权限用于搜索并连接储能设备；Android 11 及以下系统要求开启定位权限才能扫描蓝牙设备，这是系统限制。

本 APP 不读取、不存储、不上传任何位置信息。

Bluetooth permission is used to scan and connect to the energy storage device. Android 11 and below requires location permission for Bluetooth scanning — this is a system requirement.

This app never reads, stores, or uploads any location data.

数据会上传到云端吗？Is my data uploaded to the cloud

不会。本 APP 无需注册登录，所有设备数据均在本机处理与保存，不做云端同步、不做信息推送。

仅在您手动点击「检查更新」时会联网读取版本信息。

No. The app requires no registration or login. All device data is processed and stored locally, with no cloud sync and no push notifications.

The app only connects to the network when you tap "Check for Updates".

设备频繁断开连接Connection keeps dropping

1. 保持手机与设备距离，避免金属遮挡或放在封闭柜体内；

2. 关闭手机的省电限制或后台清理，允许 APP 在后台保持连接；

3. 重新进入设备详情页会自动尝试重连，仍失败可重启手机蓝牙。

1. Keep the phone close to the device and avoid metal shielding or enclosed cabinets;

2. Disable battery restrictions or background cleanup for this app;

3. Re-entering the device detail page reconnects automatically; if not, restart phone Bluetooth.

修改参数为什么要输入密码？Why is a password required

参数配置、功能开关、设备校准会直接影响电池的安全与寿命，因此保存前需要输入验证密码以防误操作。

密码请以随机说明书或供货方提供的为准；如有疑问可邮件联系我们。

Parameter settings, function switches, and device calibration directly affect battery safety and lifespan, so a verification password is required before saving.

Please refer to the manual or your supplier for the password, or email us.

电量/SOC 显示与实际电量不一致SOC does not match the actual charge

SOC 等数据由电池 BMS 上报，APP 仅做展示与记录。

若长期偏差较大，可先让电池充满并静置一段时间，或按厂家指引在「设备校准」中执行校准。

SOC and other values are reported by the battery BMS; the app only displays them.

If the deviation persists, fully charge the battery and let it rest, or run calibration under "Device Calibration" as instructed by the manufacturer.

固件升级失败或中断Firmware upgrade failed or interrupted

1. 升级过程请保持手机靠近设备、不要切到后台或锁屏；

2. 升级中断后设备通常会保留原固件，可重新连接并再次升级；

3. 若升级后设备无法连接，请停止操作并尽快邮件联系我们。

1. Keep the phone close to the device during the upgrade and avoid switching apps or locking the screen;

2. If interrupted, the device usually keeps the previous firmware — reconnect and upgrade again;

3. If the device cannot connect after an upgrade, stop operating and email us.

如何管理已保存的设备？Managing saved devices

在首页长按设备卡片可以删除该设备；点击设备卡片即可连接并查看数据。

建议同一时间只连接一台设备，避免多台设备同时通讯造成数据混乱。

Long-press a device card on the home screen to remove it; tap a card to connect and view data.

We recommend connecting one device at a time to avoid communication conflicts.
